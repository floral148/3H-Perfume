3H PERFUME WEBSITE
===================

Files:
- index.html : complete responsive website.

How to use:
1. Open index.html in Chrome to preview the website.
2. Replace the sample WhatsApp number +923000000000 in index.html with your real WhatsApp number.
3. Replace the sample phone/email and product names/prices with your real details.
4. Upload index.html to your hosting public_html folder to publish it.

The site is responsive for mobile and desktop and includes Home, Collection, About and Contact/WhatsApp order sections.
